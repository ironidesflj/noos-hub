01 — Manifesto

Three lines. The rest is context.

The noosphere is the sphere of human thought — the layer of collective knowledge that envelops the planet and to which every mind contributes. The concept was coined by Teilhard de Chardin and Vernadsky. Wikipedia is the most evident realization of the noosphere to date; certification prep is the next space that can work exactly like this.

“Wikipedia was a realization of the noosphere. This is another.”
Noos Manifesto — 1 of 3
“Reading the Noosphere requires no account. Writing to it does.”
Noos Manifesto — 2 of 3
“Whoever passed becomes myelin for whoever comes next — every explanation at a sticking point speeds up everyone’s path after it.”
Noos Manifesto — 3 of 3

The manifesto has existed in Portuguese and English since day one. The brand is never translated — “Noos” is identical in every language; it is the concept “noosphere” that flexes.

03 — Contribute

Three ways to help.

For years Wikipedia carried the most honest sentence on the internet: it would not carry advertising, it would have readers who keep it. Noos adopts the mechanics without copying the anxiety — knowledge, code or money.

Knowledge

Whoever passed the exam writes where they got stuck. Every explanation at a sticking point speeds up everyone’s path after it.

discussions open in Phase 2

Code

Open repositories with labeled issues — always with a good first issue in sight.

github.com/ironidesflj/noos-hub

Money

Pix for Brazil, where the fee is zero. GitHub Sponsors for the rest of the world, no platform fee on a personal account.

Pix c8cf26ac-db17-4d7d-a268-1bdb5c4f6b5c · GitHub Sponsors

The ask never stands between the student and the answer — even less than an ad would.
Whoever dismisses it does not see it again for 90 days.
The language is fact, not commotion: “domain renewal costs this much per year; if you can, help”.
The expectation is honest: at first, donations cover small costs — the plan does not depend on them to exist.

Contribute lives on the hub pages — never inside a mock exam.

04 — Feedback

The board is public.

Community Feedback is the public board where the project listens to its users: ideas, problems, requests for new exams and criticism of Noos itself — addressed to the founder, who answers and decides with the board open.

GitHub Discussions

Free, public, transparent and moderable, with visible status on every request: open, under review, done.

Feedback board

arriving with Phase 2

When the volume justifies it, the board is promoted to a native product with voting — the Phase 3 path. Until then, Feedback lives where the community already is: public, indexable and open to anyone, no account needed to read.
